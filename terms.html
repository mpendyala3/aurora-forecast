Terms

Simple terms for a simple site.

Use Aurora Hunt as a helpful forecast and planning tool. Don’t abuse the forms or overload the services it relies on.

Acceptable use

Use the site lawfully and avoid automated abuse, scraping, or attempts to disrupt the service.

Third-party services

Space weather comes from NOAA, weather from Open-Meteo, video from YouTube and form delivery from Formspree. Each has its own terms, and any of them can be unavailable at times.

Limitations

  • Forecasts are guidance, not a guarantee — aurora and weather are unpredictable
  • Do not rely on the site for safety decisions; winter travel needs its own planning
  • Tour operators are independent businesses; we do not vouch for or sell their services
  • Features may change or be withdrawn over time
