Cookies

One small cookie, no maze.

Aurora Hunt sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Here is everything the site can store in your browser.

What is stored

  • aurora_consent_session (session cookie) — remembers that you answered the privacy banner; deleted when you close the browser
  • aurora-consent-v1 (local storage) — your consent choice
  • aurora-forecast-v1 (local storage, opt-in only) — theme, alert threshold, map view and chosen location
  • Offline cache — a copy of the site’s own files so it loads fast and works offline

Why

  • So the privacy banner is not shown on every page
  • So your location and theme are still there next visit, if you asked for that
  • So the page opens instantly on a slow connection
Pressing play on a webcam loads a YouTube player in privacy-enhanced mode, which may set its own storage. Nothing from YouTube loads before you press play. See the privacy policy for the full list of services.
