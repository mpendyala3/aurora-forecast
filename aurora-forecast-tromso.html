      When to go

      The season runs from late August to early April. From late November to mid-January the Sun does not rise at all, so it is dark enough for aurora from mid-afternoon. From about 20 May to 22 July the midnight sun makes the aurora impossible to see.

      What Kp you need

      Tromsø is close enough to the auroral oval that Kp 0–2 is usually enough. On most clear, dark nights there is a chance, and anything above Kp 3 can be spectacular.

      Where to watch

      In town, Telegrafbukta beach and the lake Prestvannet are the easy options. For darker skies head to Ersfjordbotn or elsewhere on Kvaløya, 30–40 minutes by car.

      Local tip

      Tromsø is coastal and the weather changes fast. When the coast is clouded over, local guides often drive inland toward Skibotn and the Finnish border, where the air is drier and skies clear more often.
