About

Built for people chasing the northern lights.

Aurora Hunt answers one question as honestly as it can: is it worth going outside tonight? It reads live space weather, real cloud cover and the darkness of your sky, and turns them into a single hour-by-hour chance.

What it does

It combines the planetary Kp index, NOAA’s Kp forecast, solar wind speed and Bz with hourly cloud cover and the Sun’s true position at your coordinates. The result is a visibility chance for any place on Earth, a best viewing window, a 27-day outlook and a live map of the auroral oval.

Why it exists

Most aurora tools show raw numbers and leave the interpretation to you, or bury the answer under ads and sign-ups. Aurora Hunt keeps the useful parts on one page, explains them in plain language, and never asks for an account.

Where the data comes from

  • NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center — observed Kp, 3-day Kp forecast, 27-day outlook, solar wind and the OVATION aurora model
  • Open-Meteo — cloud cover, time zones and place search (CC BY 4.0)
  • Sunrise, sunset, twilight and moon phase are calculated in your browser

How the chance is calculated

  • Reach — your geomagnetic latitude against how far the oval extends at the current or forecast Kp
  • Energy — live solar wind speed and southward Bz raise the score
  • Darkness — the Sun must be well below the horizon; 12° or more is ideal
  • Sky — cloud cover scales the chance toward zero

Principles

  • Free, with no account and no advertising
  • Honest when data is missing: if a feed is down the page says so instead of inventing numbers
  • Private by default: no analytics, no third-party fonts, video only loads when you press play
  • A forecast is guidance, not a guarantee — the sky has the final say
