      When to go

      The season runs from mid-August to mid-April. Autumn offers milder nights; midwinter offers the longest darkness.

      What Kp you need

      Whitehorse is close enough to the auroral oval that Kp 0–2 is usually enough. On most clear, dark nights there is a chance, and anything above Kp 3 can be spectacular.

      Where to watch

      Fish Lake Road and the Chadburn Lake area are both a short drive from town and well clear of city lights.

      Local tip

      Mountains surround Whitehorse, so choose a spot with an open northern horizon — a frozen lake is ideal.
