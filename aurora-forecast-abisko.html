      When to go

      The viewing season runs from September to late March. Midwinter brings the polar night, with only a few hours of twilight around midday.

      What Kp you need

      Abisko is close enough to the auroral oval that Kp 0–2 is usually enough. On most clear, dark nights there is a chance, and anything above Kp 3 can be spectacular.

      Where to watch

      The shore of Lake Torneträsk and the trails of Abisko National Park give open views north. The Aurora Sky Station on Mount Nuolja is reached by chairlift and sits above most local light.

      Local tip

      The mountains to the west wring moisture out of Atlantic weather, leaving a patch of clearer sky over the lake that locals call the “blue hole”. It is why Abisko often has stars when the Norwegian coast is clouded over.
