Privacy policy

Private by default.

Aurora Hunt has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. The forecast is calculated in your browser. This page lists exactly what is stored and which services your browser talks to. Last updated 16 November 2026.

Stored in your browser Services contacted Forms Location Your choices

Stored in your browser

Your consent choice is kept in local storage, plus one session cookie so the banner does not reappear while you browse. Only if you tick “Remember preferences” do we also save your theme, alert threshold and chosen location in local storage. Nothing is stored on a server.

Services your browser contacts

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and, like any web host, sees your IP address
  • NOAA SWPC — space weather data; receives your IP address only
  • Open-Meteo — weather and place search; receives the coordinates or place name you look up
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) — only after you press play on a webcam
  • Formspree — only if you submit a form

Forms

The alert, contact and operator forms are delivered to our inbox by Formspree. The alert form sends your email address, your threshold and the location you selected, rounded to about one kilometre. We use this only to reply or send aurora alerts, and never sell or share it. To have it removed, use the contact form.

Location

“Use my location” asks your browser for a position only when you press it. The coordinates are rounded to about one kilometre before they are used, and are remembered only if you opted in to saved preferences.

Your choices

  • Choose “Essential only” in the privacy banner to store no preferences at all
  • Reopen the banner any time from “Privacy choices” in the homepage footer
  • Clear this site’s data in your browser settings to reset everything
We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or third-party fonts. If this ever changes, this page will say so first.
